Terms, in plain words

For a Bloom Days planting plan. Questions? kaitlin@bloomdays.co

What you get

A planting plan for one bed: one planting area you see as a single space, like the bed along the front walk. Two separate spots are two plans. Your packet has six documents: a palette with a reason under every plant, a bloom calendar from April to October, a plan on a 1 ft grid with quantities and pot sizes, a sourcing list, an install guide and a care plan.

What's not included

The plants, the planting and a site visit. You or your installer plant it from the install guide.

How it works

  • You book and pay $250. I email you to set up our call.
  • We talk for 15 minutes on a video call. Before it, you send a short walk-around video and rough measurements.
  • I show you your palette as pictures, and you tell me what to change.
  • Your packet arrives within three weeks of our call.

Changes

One round of changes to your palette is included. After that, each extra round is $75, and changes after the packet arrives count as one.

Refunds

If either of us thinks it isn't the right fit after our call, you get a full refund. Once you've seen your palette the work has started, so the $250 isn't refundable. Gift cards never expire, aren't refundable, and can be passed on.

Photos

I'd love to photograph your garden as it grows, and I only share photos with your permission. Never your name or your street, and I'll ask before naming your town. Say no and you get the same plan.
