# The $50 search test — paste-ready

**What this test is for:** finding out whether anyone in Fairfield County is searching
for this at all, and building the machinery so March 1 is a button rather than a project.
**September is the worst month of the year to measure garden-design demand** — search
peaks Feb–May. Read the result as a FLOOR, not a forecast. Do not conclude anything
about demand from it.

---

## 0 · Before spending a dollar — conversion tracking

Without this you learn how many clicks you bought and nothing about what they did.

1. In **Formspree** → your form → Settings → **Redirect URL** → `https://bloomdays.co/thanks.html`
2. Push `thanks.html` (already written, sitting in `Site/`):
   `git add . && git commit -m "thanks page" && git push`
3. In **Google Ads** → Goals → Conversions → New → **Website** → set up a
   **Page view** conversion on URL contains `thanks.html`. Count as **Primary**.

A form fill now equals one pageview of a page nobody can reach any other way.

## 5 · Reading it — the only three outcomes that matter

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **No impressions** | Nobody is searching these terms in these towns. In September this is probably the season, not the market. | Do not spend more. Second channel in October. The real read is February. |
| **Impressions, no clicks** | People search, your ad does not appeal. A copy problem. | Rewrite headlines once. Cheapest possible fix. |
| **Clicks, no form fills** | People want this, your page does not convert. A site problem. | The best of the three outcomes — demand is real and the fix is yours. |

**Adjust keywords ONCE, in week two. Then leave it alone.** A week of data is barely
data, and two changes in two weeks teaches you nothing.
